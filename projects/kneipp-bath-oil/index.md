A series of illustrations for bath oil packaging, inspired by calming spa rituals, delicate fragrances and the soft border between sleep and waking. Each scent — Cedar, Lavender, Water Mint and Wild Peony — has its own landscape and mood, from the first colour sketches to the finished bottles and boxes.
