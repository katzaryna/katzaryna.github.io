A romantic, atmospheric invitation for “Museum for Two”, the museum's exclusive after-hours experience: a private tour of the poet's apartment followed by tea at sunset. The card opens like the museum's real wrought-iron gate, and inside, soft and nostalgic illustrations carry the date, the time and the names of the invited couple.
