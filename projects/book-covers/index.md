A series of covers for books about ordinary days that feel like a small holiday. The covers share a hand-lettered title and a soft, sunny palette, while the scenes change: a bike ride through a meadow, a slow afternoon with a guitar and a dog, a picnic high in the mountains. Small spot illustrations of everyday joys complete the set.
